Your app's user manual,
written by your coding agent.

Type /guidegen in Claude Code, Codex, Cursor or opencode. GuideGen reads your code, runs your app, explores every screen, captures redacted, annotated screenshots and writes a branded manual in Word and HTML.

/plugin marketplace add guidegen/guidegen
/plugin install guidegen@guidegen

What you get

  • Getting Started, How-To Tasks (8-15, ranked by evidence from your E2E tests and forms), a Screen Reference for every reachable screen, and Troubleshooting built from your validation messages.
  • Your logo, product name and brand colors, detected automatically.
  • Secrets blurred before anything touches disk, destructive buttons refused, localhost backends only.
  • /guidegen --update keeps your edits and re-captures only the screens whose code changed.
