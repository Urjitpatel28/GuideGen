    Getting Started

    Acme Shop is where you manage your products, customers and orders. This manual shows you how to do everyday jobs step by step, and explains every screen.

    Before you begin

    • A computer with an up-to-date web browser (Microsoft Edge, Google Chrome, Firefox or Safari).
    • The Acme Shop web address and the email and password your administrator gave you.

    Starting the application

    Open your web browser and go to the Acme Shop address. The Sign in to Acme Shop page appears.

    Signing in

    Type your Email and Password, then click Sign in. If you see Invalid email or password, check both and try again.

    A quick tour

    After you sign in you see the Dashboard. The menu on the left takes you to Products, Orders, Customers and Settings. Click Sign out at the bottom of the menu when you are finished.

    Sign in to Acme Shop

    Get into Acme Shop with your email and password.

    1. 1

      Open Acme Shop in your web browser. The Sign in to Acme Shop page appears.

      Screen: Sign in

    2. 2

      In Email, type your email address.

      Screen: Sign in

    3. 3

      In Password, type your password.

      Screen: Sign in

    4. 4

      Click Sign in. The Dashboard opens.

      Screen: Dashboard

    Create an order

    Record an order for a customer and take the items out of stock.

    1. 1

      In the menu, click Orders.

      Screen: Dashboard

    2. 2

      Click New order.

      Screen: Orders

    3. 3

      In Customer, choose who the order is for.

      Screen: New order

    4. 4

      In Product, choose what they are buying.

      Screen: New order

    5. 5

      In Quantity, type how many.

      Screen: New order

    6. 6

      Click Create order. The new order opens with the status Open.

      Screen: Order details

    Troubleshooting

    MessageWhy it happensHow to fix it
    Invalid email or password

    The email or password does not match your account.

    Check for typing mistakes and that Caps Lock is off, then try again. Ask your administrator if you have forgotten your password.

    Price must be greater than 0

    The Price box is empty, 0 or not a number.

    Type a price such as 9.99.

    Stock cannot be negative

    The stock count is below zero or not a number.

    Type 0 or a positive whole number.

    Select a customer

    No customer was chosen in Customer.

    Choose a customer. If they are not in the list, add them on the Customers page first.

    Quantity must be greater than 0

    The Quantity is 0, empty or not a number.

    Type 1 or more.

    Not enough stock for this quantity

    You asked for more items than are in stock.

    Lower the quantity, or correct the stock count on the product's page first.

    Email is not valid

    The email address is missing a part such as @ or the domain.

    Type a full address, for example name@company.com.

    Low-stock threshold must be between 0 and 1000

    The number is outside the allowed range.

    Type a whole number from 0 to 1000.
