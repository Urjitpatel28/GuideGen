    Getting Started

    ClientDesk keeps your customer contacts, calls, emails and meetings in one place, and shows the value of your sales pipeline at a glance.

    Before you begin

    • A Windows 10 or Windows 11 PC with ClientDesk installed.
    • Your ClientDesk user name and password.

    Starting the application

    Open the Start menu and click ClientDesk. The Sign in to ClientDesk window appears.

    Signing in

    Type your User name and Password, then click Sign in.

    A quick tour

    The main window has three tabs. Customers lists your contacts, Activities shows your calls, emails and meetings, and Dashboard shows your pipeline. The File, Customers and Help menus are at the top.

    Add a customer

    Start tracking a new contact.

    1. 1

      On the Customers tab, click New customer.

      Screen: Customers tab

    2. 2

      Type the contact's Name.

      Screen: New customer window

    3. 3

      Type their Company.

      Screen: New customer window

    4. 4

      Type their Email address.

      Screen: New customer window

    5. 5

      Choose a Status.

      Screen: New customer window

    6. 6

      Type the expected Annual value.

      Screen: New customer window

    7. 7

      Click Save. The customer appears in the list.

      Screen: Customers tab

    Screens used: Customers tab, New customer window

    Log a call or meeting

    Keep a record of what you discussed with a customer.

    1. 1

      On the Customers tab, click the customer.

      Screen: Customers tab

    2. 2

      Click Log activity.

      Screen: Customers tab

    3. 3

      Choose the Type of activity.

      Screen: Log activity window

    4. 4

      Type what you discussed in Notes.

      Screen: Log activity window

    5. 5

      Click Save activity. It appears on the Activities tab.

      Screen: Activities tab

    Screens used: Customers tab, Log activity window, Activities tab

    Change the default status for new customers

    Save time when most new contacts start at the same stage.

    1. 1

      Click the File menu.

      Screen: Customers tab

    2. 2

      Click Options....

      Screen: Customers tab

    3. 3

      Choose a Default status.

      Screen: Options window

    4. 4

      Click OK.

      Screen: Customers tab

    Screens used: Customers tab, Options window

    Delete a customer

    Remove a contact you no longer need.

    1. 1

      On the Customers tab, click the customer.

      Screen: Customers tab

    2. 2

      Click Delete, then click Yes. Their logged activities are kept.

      Screen: Customers tab

    Troubleshooting

    MessageWhy it happensHow to fix it
    The user name or password is incorrect.

    The sign-in details do not match an account.

    Check both and that Caps Lock is off.

    Enter the customer's name.

    The Name box is empty.

    Type the contact's name.

    Enter a valid email address.

    The email address is incomplete.

    Type a full address such as name@company.com.

    A customer with this email address already exists.

    Two customers cannot share an email address.

    Search for the existing customer and edit them instead.

    Annual value must be a number of 0 or more.

    The value has letters or is negative.

    Type a number such as 15000, without currency symbols.

    Select a customer first.

    You clicked Edit or Delete without choosing a customer.

    Click a customer in the list first.

    Choose a customer for this activity.

    No customer was chosen in Customer.

    Choose who the activity was with.

    Notes cannot be longer than 500 characters.

    The notes are too long.

    Shorten the notes or split them into two activities.
